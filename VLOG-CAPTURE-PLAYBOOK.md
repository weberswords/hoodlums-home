# Classroom OS — Vlog & Capture Playbook

**A 5-day field strategy for turning the week into a district pitch.**
Las Vegas · June 1–5, 2026 · Legacy High School · The Intelligent Hoodlums

---

## 0. The North Star (read this first)

We are not making "event recap" content. Every frame we shoot this week is **evidence
for one argument**:

> We took CCSD teachers who *wanted* to be here and, in five mornings, helped them
> build a classroom that runs whether they're in the room or not — and that's exactly
> the kind of learner experience the **Portrait of a Nevada Learner** is asking for.

Two things come out the other side of this week:

1. **The Pitch Film** — a 3–5 min cut we can play for a principal, a superintendent,
   or a district decision-maker. The "why you should hire us."
2. **The White Paper** — the research-based, logical document: *this is what we did,
   why we did it, why it makes sense, and how it runs on the tools already in your
   ecosystem* (Google, NotebookLM, Gems). The vlog is the **qualitative** spine of
   that paper; the [Pulse Check survey](./SURVEY-SETUP.md) is the **quantitative**
   spine. Same week, two kinds of proof.

If a clip doesn't move one of those two deliverables forward, we don't need it.

---

## 1. The One Question (the throughline)

Every before/after vlog is a reaction to **the same question**, asked from a different
angle each day. This is what makes five days of phone footage edit together into one story:

> ### "Can we build a classroom that runs without the teacher in it — and make a district see why that matters?"

- The **Before** vlog (morning) answers: *What are we trying to prove today, and what
  would make today a win?*
- The **After** vlog (evening) answers: *What did we actually capture today that proves
  it — and what surprised us?*

That's the format. Don't overthink it on camera. State the question, take the bet,
come back and show the proof.

---

## 2. The Daily Vlog Format (repeatable — same 3 beats every time)

Shoot these on a phone, vertical, handheld, talking to camera. 60–90 seconds each.
The discipline is that the **structure never changes**, so the series feels intentional.

### ☀️ Morning "Before" vlog — 3 beats
1. **The Question** — say today's question out loud (from the table in §3).
2. **The Stakes** — one sentence on why a teacher *or a district* should care today.
   (Tie it to the sub-plan reality or to a Portrait theme — see §5.)
3. **The Bet** — "Here's what we're going to build / prove by 12:30." Name the artifact.

### 🌙 Evening "After" vlog — 3 beats
1. **The Callback** — re-ask the morning question.
2. **The Proof** — what got built, plus the single best thing a teacher said today.
3. **The Tease** — one line pointing at tomorrow. (On Thu, tease the Friday test hard.)

> **Hosting tip:** one consistent host fronts every vlog (continuity), but pull a
> *different teacher* into the After vlog each day. By Friday the cohort has narrated
> their own transformation — that's far more convincing than us narrating it for them.

## 4. The Friday Money Shot — the Gem Swap

This is the single most important thing we film all week, because it's the literal proof
of the thesis. From the plan:

> On Friday, each teacher **swaps their Gem with another person**. That person — who has
> never seen this content — is handed the Gem and has to **lead it through the 5 tasks**.

This is "does it work without you?" made visible. Film it like a stress test, not a
celebration:

- **Cold open**: a teacher hands their laptop/Gem to a stranger-crew. "I built this.
  You've never seen it. Run my class."
- **The attempt**: shoot over-the-shoulder of the *substitute* working the Gem through
  the 5 tasks. Get their face when it actually works.
- **The verdict**: turn the camera on the *builder* watching someone else successfully
  run their classroom without them. **That reaction shot is the close of the Pitch Film.**
- **The soundbite to land**: ask the substitute on camera — *"Could you have run that
  class today with nothing but what they built?"* Their unscripted "yeah, actually" is
  worth more than anything we could say about ourselves.

If you get one thing on camera this week, get this clean (steady, good audio, both faces).

## 5. Portrait of a Nevada Learner — the district hook

A superintendent doesn't buy "cool teacher PD." They buy **alignment to the framework
they're already accountable to.** The Portrait of a Nevada Learner is built on four
themes — and our five days hit all four. Say these words on camera; they're the
district's own language.

| Portrait theme | How Classroom OS proves it | Where to capture it |
|----------------|----------------------------|---------------------|
| **Know themselves** | Day 1 Source Code — teachers naming what they believe about learning | Mon — Circle Map + Source Code soundbites |
| **Connect with others** | The **crew** model — a chemistry teacher catching a blind spot for an English teacher | Tue/Fri — crew feedback, Gallery Walk |
| **Contribute their skills** | Teachers building real tools (Gems, sub plans) that serve actual students | Thu — the build; artifacts that ship to September |
| **Thrive through challenges** | The sub-plan / Gem Swap — a system that holds when the person isn't there | Fri — the Gem Swap stress test |

And the **durable skills** the Portrait names — communicate, think critically and
creatively, work ethic and persistence — are visibly on display in every crew session.
**The pitch line to record to camera:** *"The Portrait of a Nevada Learner asks us to
help kids know themselves, connect, contribute, and thrive. This week, the teachers did
exactly that — and built classrooms that ask the same of their students. This philosophy
applies to everything."*

> Sources for Portrait language:
> [nvfutureoflearning.org/portrait](https://www.nvfutureoflearning.org/portrait) ·
> [KnowledgeWorks: Building a Portrait of a Nevada Learner](https://knowledgeworks.org/resources/nevada-portrait-learner-community-vision/) ·
> [NDE Future of Learning Network](https://doe.nv.gov/offices/office-of-teaching-and-learning/future-of-learning-network)

## 6. The Soundbite Bank (the lines that actually sell)

The pitch is won on teachers' own words, not ours. Pull people aside for 30 seconds and
ask these. Get name + grade + subject on camera first ("I'm ___, I teach ___ at ___").

**For teachers — ask across the week:**
- "What did you walk in believing about PD, and what changed?"
- "What did you build today that you'll actually open in August?"
- "Finish this: *Before this week, my sub plan was ___. Now it's ___.*"
- "What could you build with this that has nothing to do with your subject?"
  *(captures the "applies to everything" claim)*
- "Why did you choose to spend your summer morning here?"
  *(these are CCSD teachers who **wanted** to attend — say that on camera)*

**For Admin Observers (the 4 daily seats) — gold for the pitch:**
- "You've sat through a lot of PD. What's different about what you watched today?"
- "What would it mean for your building if every teacher had a system like this?"
- "How does what you saw connect to the Portrait of a Nevada Learner?"

**The closer (Friday, to anyone):**
- "If you were a principal, would you send your staff to this? Why?"

## 7. Shot List — what to grab beyond the talking heads

B-roll is what makes a 3-minute cut feel like a film instead of a webcam. Each day, bank:

- **Hands working** — markers on maps, typing into NotebookLM, a finger scrolling a Gem.
- **Faces thinking** — the quiet moment before someone writes their Source Code.
- **Crew in motion** — four people leaning over one laptop, the argument, the laugh.
- **The room** — wide establishing shot of Legacy High, the cohort working, the energy.
- **Artifacts close-up** — the maps on the wall, the screen of a live Gem, the sub plan.
- **The QR / brand moments** — Pulse Check QR codes, the badge, anything branded TIH.
- **Arrivals & goodbyes** — teachers walking in Monday vs. walking out Friday (the arc).

> **The transformation diptych:** shoot the *same teacher* in the same spot on Monday
> morning and Friday afternoon. Same framing. That before/after cut sells itself.

## 9. Logistics & discipline (so we don't lose the gold)

- **Shoot vertical for reels, but grab key moments horizontal too** — the Pitch Film and
  any district presentation wants landscape. When in doubt, shoot the money moments
  (§4) in **landscape**.
- **Audio is the whole game.** A $20 lav or even AirPods beats built-in mic in a noisy
  room. Bad audio kills a testimonial faster than bad video.
- **Consent.** Anyone identifiable on camera signs a one-line media release — especially
  admin observers. No release, no face in the pitch. Have a paper/photo log at the door.
- **No students** unless you have explicit district + parent consent. For this cohort
  (teachers/adults) you're clear; keep it that way.
- **File hygiene, end of every day:** dump to one folder named
  `YYYY-MM-DD_Day0X_ClassroomOS`. **Back up to the cloud before you sleep.** Phones get
  dropped in pools in Las Vegas.
- **Two clips minimum per day** that you'd be happy to post *that night* — momentum on
  social during the week builds the audience that watches the Pitch Film after.
