The Intelligent Hoodlums · The Crew

Who’s behind
the mask.

Two instructional designers who had seen too many schools sold temporal solutions by people who had never taught a single period. We built something different. Everything we make is pressure-tested by people who have taught it, at schools where Tuesday is the hardest day.

Co-founder

Mike Lang

Creative Direction · Curriculum Architecture · Instructional Design.

Mike is an Apple Distinguished Educator, a PBS Digital Innovator, and a Heart of Education Award winner. He has taught for eighteen years across Mississippi, Taiwan, and Las Vegas, and he serves as Chief Innovation Program Officer for ed.Xtraordinary, powered by Teach For America. Apple featured his students’ civic-action project in its newsroom. He teaches that every student should understand the role they can play in changing the world, and that belief is the engine behind everything the Hoodlums build.

Known for: Apple Distinguished Educator. PBS Digital Innovator. Heart of Education Award. Chief Innovation Program Officer, ed.Xtraordinary (Teach For America).

Co-founder

Webs

Software Developer · Workshop Facilitation · Instructional Design.

Webs pairs ten years in Clark County classrooms with doctoral work in the learning sciences at UNLV, a master’s in Educational Leadership, and a second career as a software developer. She builds the systems and the tools that make the Hoodlums’ work survive contact with a real classroom, and she leads the professional development that puts them in teachers’ hands.

Known for: Doctoral work in the learning sciences (UNLV). Master’s in Educational Leadership. Clark County classroom teaching and curriculum design. Software developer and founder.
