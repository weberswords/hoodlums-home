The Intelligent Hoodlums · The Crew

Who’s behind
the mask.

Two instructional designers who had seen too many schools sold temporal solutions by people who had never taught a single period. We built something different. Everything we make is pressure-tested by people who have taught it, at schools where Tuesday is the hardest day.

Co-founder

Mike Lang

Creative Direction · Curriculum Architecture · Instructional Design.

Mike is an Apple Distinguished Educator, a PBS Digital Innovator (opens in new tab), and a Heart of Education Award (opens in new tab) winner. He has taught for eighteen years across Mississippi, Taiwan, and Las Vegas, and he has taken the stage everywhere from district classrooms to SXSW EDU. Apple featured his students’ civic-action project in its newsroom (opens in new tab), and the Las Vegas Review-Journal (opens in new tab) told the story too. He has even sat down with Dr. Henry Louis Gates Jr. on how to teach the Civil War through a new lens (opens in new tab). He teaches that every student should understand the role they can play in changing the world, and that belief is the engine behind everything the Hoodlums build.

Known for: Apple Distinguished Educator. PBS Digital Innovator. Heart of Education Award. SXSW presenter.

Co-founder

Webs

Digital Product Design · Workshop Facilitation · Instructional Design.

Webs is an Apple Distinguished Educator and a digital product designer who pairs ten years in Clark County classrooms with a master’s in Educational Leadership and doctoral work in the learning sciences at UNLV, where she is ABD and finishing her dissertation. She builds the systems and the tools that make the Hoodlums’ work survive contact with a real classroom, and she leads the professional development that puts them in teachers’ hands.

Known for: Apple Distinguished Educator. Digital product designer. Doctoral candidate (ABD) in the learning sciences, UNLV. Master’s in Educational Leadership. Clark County classroom teaching and curriculum design.
