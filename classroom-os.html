Chapter 01

What is Classroom OS?

An operating system is the layer that decides how everything else runs. Most teachers inherit one by accident — built from old habits, district mandates, and whatever survived the last in-service. Classroom OS is five working days to rebuild that layer on purpose: the routines, the structures, and the small decisions that make the rest of the year possible. No theory. No binders. Tools you'll use the morning after.
