# Content Tiering — What goes where (why → what → how)

A reusable guardrail for deciding what belongs on the public **webpage**, in the
gated **white paper**, and what stays inside the **paid PD** only. Use this when
writing the white paper or any new public page.

> Governing principle: the **executional HOW** — templates, facilitation moves,
> live adaptation — never leaves the paid room. The white paper reveals the
> *architecture* of the method (so it reads as rigorous and worth buying) without
> ever being a **runnable playbook**. A reader should finish it thinking
> "this is rigorous and I want it," NOT "great, I can run this Monday."

---

## The three tiers

| | **Webpage** (appetizer) | **White paper** (the meal) | **The PD** (the kitchen) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience | Cold — anyone with the link | Warm — a decision-maker you've engaged | Paid — in the room |
| Reveals | **WHY** it matters + **THAT** it worked | **WHAT** the method is, in depth | **HOW** to actually do it |
| Job | Create desire + credibility | Justify the investment; prove rigor | Deliver the transformation |
| Access | Public | Gated (lead capture) | Paid only |

---

## What lands in each tier

**WEBPAGE (why / that):**
- Mirror thesis ("what if the machine wasn't the enemy? what if it was a mirror?")
- Testimonials, the human story (anonymized case study)
- Outcome data — the headline Pulse numbers (rating, advocacy, the shift)
- The A/B **result** ("it reflects what you put in") — not the test design
- The vibe: clear-not-clever, psychological safety, AI hands-on from day one
- The four pillar NAMES as brand signature (Source Code · Architecture ·
  Scheduler · Applications) — names only, not the build steps

**WHITE PAPER (what, in depth):**
- The framework architecture + WHY it's sequenced this way
- The theory and how it's interleaved: cognitive load (Sweller), Chi, Schlechty
  [confirm citations before printing]
- Full Pulse methodology + complete Monday→Friday data + the case study in full
- The A/B test as a **diagnostic** (design + what it reveals)
- The human → analog → digital scaffold as a named method
- Enough to be credible + teachable-sounding; still not copy-paste runnable

**THE PD ONLY (how — never published):**
- The templates: Source Code doc structure, Architecture tool, Brand Kit
- The Thinking Maps as scaffolds (Circle / Bubble / Flow) and how they're used
- The prompt-building method + any prompt library
- "Slopped" mechanics, the curveballs, the scaffolds, exact facilitation script
- The tool chain how-to (Course Brain → Gems → Google Classroom, step by step)
- Live adaptation / facilitation moves — the actual moat
