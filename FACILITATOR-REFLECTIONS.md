## 2026-06-05 — End-of-week brain dump

Possibly the **best professional development session we've ever done.**

**Zero to hero.** **Dawn — 5th grade teacher at Snyder ES** — went from zero to
hero in the span of a week. Watching that arc was the thing.

**We opened by making them question who they are as teachers.** Started the week
with identity, not tools — who are you as a teacher? But we gave them one early
piece that signaled where we were headed: from **day one they were touching AI.**
The activity where they had to **draw a picture, describe it, and then put it
into Adobe Firefly.** That told them *we knew what we were doing* and that AI
was real here — but AI was never the base of it.

**We ran the week the same way we were teaching them to run their classrooms:**
- Start with the **human**, then move to **analog**, then move to **digital** —
  whatever digital looked like in the moment.
- That structure let us **layer in pedagogical strategies** the same way.
- We gave them tools — **Pomodoro timers, homofocus.io** — but more importantly
  **demonstrated how they'd use those tools in their own classrooms.** Tools in
  service of the system, modeled live.

**Clear rather than clever.** We were deliberate about this, and I think that's
what made the experience actually resonate with people.

**The peak: the A/B test (yesterday).** Same prompt to **vanilla Gemini**, then
the same prompt to **Gemini seated with their Classroom OS.** That was probably
the high point of the whole week — the moment they could *see* that all the work
had been worth it. They could start producing documents almost effortlessly that
**matched their voice and their classroom dynamics.**
